What we bring

What's at Our Table at Every Show

Our inventory rotates before every show — we post previews on Instagram and Facebook before each event so you know what's coming. Here's what you'll typically find at our table.

Pokémon Singles

Raw singles from modern and vintage sets — alt arts, full arts, special illustration rares, holo rares, and more. Priced against current market data so you know you're getting a fair deal. New cards added before every show.

Graded Slabs

PSA and BGS graded Pokémon cards across modern and vintage sets. We carry a rotating selection of slabs at every show — from affordable mid-grades to high-end PSA 10s and BGS gems. Ask us about anything specific and we'll let you know if it's in the binder.

Sealed Product

Booster packs, Elite Trainer Boxes, and select sealed sets at competitive show pricing. Stock rotates event to event. Follow us before the show to see what sealed product we're bringing.

We Buy at Shows Too

Bring your collection to the show and sell it on the spot. We make offers at the table — no appointment needed at shows. Singles, bulk, slabs, sealed product — bring what you've got and we'll take a look.

About the shows

Pokémon Card Shows in and Around Owensboro, KY

The Owensboro area has a growing card show scene with events happening regularly throughout the year. Here's what you need to know about the shows we attend and the region's card show landscape.

Owensboro Card Shows

Owensboro hosts regular trading card shows throughout the year. These are the home shows for us — our table is fully stocked and we're here to buy, sell, and trade. The local collector community turns out in force and it's a great environment for all levels of collector.

Evansville Card Shows

Just across the state line in Evansville, IN, the Armory Card Show draws collectors from Southern Indiana and Western Kentucky. We vend at the Evansville show regularly — it's one of the stronger regional events in the area with a wide range of vendors.

Western Kentucky Shows

The Western Kentucky Collectibles Show brings together vendors from across the region. It's a broader collectibles event that includes a strong Pokémon card section — we bring a full setup and fresh inventory for every WKY show.

Can't Make a Show?

No problem — we do local meetups throughout Owensboro and Western KY year-round. Text us at (270) 883-0151 or book an appointment and we'll come to you. Buying and selling doesn't require a show.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions — Pokémon Card Shows Near Owensboro

Common questions from collectors before heading to a show.

Do you vend at every Pokémon card show near Owensboro?

We attend most of the major shows in the region but not every single event. The best way to know if we'll be at a specific show is to follow us on Instagram or Facebook — we announce every show we're attending, usually a few days in advance.

Can I sell my Pokémon cards to you at a show?

Yes — we buy collections at shows too. Bring what you have, find our table, and we'll look through everything on the spot. No appointment needed at shows. If it's a large collection, texting us ahead of time helps us be prepared to make a strong offer.

Can I put a card on hold before the show?

Absolutely. DM us on Instagram or Facebook with what you're interested in and we'll hold it at the table for you. We post inventory previews before each show so you can see what's coming — follow us so you don't miss a drop.

What payment do you accept at shows?

Cash, Venmo, CashApp, and PayPal. Cash is always welcome and the fastest option at a show. All digital payments are instant — no pending holds.

Do you ship if I can't make the show?

Yes — if you see something in our featured inventory you want but can't make the show, reach out and we can arrange shipping or a local meetup near Owensboro. DM us on Instagram or text (270) 883-0151.

How often do you update your show inventory?

Every show. We rotate inventory before each event and post previews on social so you can see what's new before you arrive. Following us on Instagram is the best way to stay current.
