How it started

We Just Really Love Pokémon

It started the way it probably did for a lot of collectors — a few packs here, a card we remembered from childhood there. Before long we had binders, boxes, and a very understanding policy about how much space "the hobby" was allowed to take up in the house.

At some point we looked around and realized we had gone a little overboard. Cards we'd bought on impulse, duplicates, sets we'd pulled chasing a specific card — a lot of it was just sitting there, not being enjoyed by anyone.

"The vast majority of what you see on our table came from our own collection."

These aren't cards we sourced from a warehouse. They're cards we pulled, traded for, hunted down, and genuinely loved. We're just passing them on to people who'll love them just as much.

Why we vend

It's About the Culture

We didn't start vending at shows to run a business. We started because card shows have a specific energy that's hard to explain if you haven't felt it — the kid who spots the exact card they've been hunting, the collector who lights up finding something for their binder, the parents buying their first pack with their kid.

That's the culture. That's why we're there.

We vendor at shows across Western Kentucky and Southern Indiana — mostly around Owensboro but we travel when the show's worth it. Come find us, dig through the binders, and say hi. We genuinely love talking cards.

Selling your cards

We Buy Collections Too

Because we've been on the collector side of things, we know what it feels like to get a lowball offer on something you care about. We don't do that. If you're looking to sell your Pokémon cards in Owensboro or anywhere in Western Kentucky, reach out — we'll give you a straight, honest answer on value and we'll come to you.

Bulk cards, graded slabs, sealed product, vintage Base Set era cards — we buy all of it. No collection is too big or too small.
